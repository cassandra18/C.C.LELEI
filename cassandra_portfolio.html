01 — Who I Am

A builder at heart,
a thinker by nature.

June '26 Graduation

I am an Electrical Engineer and a Software Developer with a deep passion for building systems that work beautifully — not just technically, but for the people who depend on them.

Through ALX's rigorous Software Engineering program, I sharpened my ability to write clean, scalable code and think like a true systems architect. The AI Career Essentials program pushed me further — into the frontier of intelligent systems and the ethics of building them responsibly.

I am the founder of LeleiMade Co. Ltd, where I am building two ventures: a Real Estate Management Software platform, and Virtual Power Plant as a Service — because clean, smart energy is not a luxury. It is infrastructure.

I come from the Kalenjin people of Kenya — a heritage of resilience, precision, and attentiveness to the world. I bring all of that to every line of code I write and every system I design.

I don't just write code. I build futures.
